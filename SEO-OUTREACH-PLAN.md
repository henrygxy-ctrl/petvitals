## Weekly Execution

1. Publish or share one answer on a relevant community thread.
   - Target: Reddit, Quora, niche pet forums, local rescue groups.
   - Rule: answer the question fully first; link only when the checklist or guide genuinely helps.

2. Pitch two resource-page links.
   - Target: rescues, shelters, dog trainers, cat behavior sites, pet boarding businesses, and veterinary blogs with resource pages.
   - Best assets: cleaning checklist, poison checklist, emergency vet checklist.

3. Pitch one expert quote or guest contribution.
   - Target topics: pet-safe cleaning, emergency vet cost planning, pet insurance quote comparison, household poison prevention.
   - Link to a guide page, not the homepage.

4. Repurpose one checklist into a visual post.
   - Target: Pinterest, Facebook groups, and short-form social.
   - Use the PDF as the call-to-action asset.
